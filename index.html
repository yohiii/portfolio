NAVIS: Autonomous Indoor Navigation and Delivery System In progress

Senior Design Project · Aug 2026 – Present

Designing an autonomous guide and delivery robot that eliminates pre-mapping and pre-configuration, enabling zero-setup deployment in any indoor environment. The robot uses real-time SLAM, guided by an uploaded floor plan, to plan routes and guide people or deliver items to a chosen room. Integrating LiDAR, a camera, and mecanum wheels into a ROS2 navigation stack for omnidirectional mobility and dynamic obstacle avoidance in previously unseen spaces.

Independent Project · Mar 2026 – Apr 2026
